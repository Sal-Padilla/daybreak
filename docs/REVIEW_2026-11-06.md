# Daybreak screen review — 6 November 2026 (v0.9.5)

Daybreak's engine received Lady_GYM's engine fixes in v0.9.4. This review covers the rest: the
screens, the database layer and navigation. Two reviewers worked in parallel — one reading the
code, one using every screen — and each fix was reproduced first, then re-checked in Chromium
at iPhone 14, iPhone SE and 320pt widths, light and dark, root text at 16, 20 and 24 px, with
the clock in California (5:20 am, 6:45 pm, 9:30 pm, the DST Sunday) and offline. Both apps were
also run in real WebKit (Safari's engine) — see Lady_GYM/docs/REVIEW_2026-11-05.md.

## Fixed

**Double taps** (reproduced with storage slowed to phone speed and taps 25–120 ms apart)
- "Start" made two sessions, and a tick logged the same set twice — even once the first save had
  re-rendered an identical button under her finger.
- "Save" in a sheet stored two measurements; "Add" in the class picker added the class twice.
- In onboarding a double tap skipped the next question (the life-stage one included) and could
  start the app twice; in the warm-up it skipped a move.
- The second tap could close a sheet that had just opened, or answer a confirm dialog unseen.

**Her data**
- A workout left without tapping Finish stayed unfinished forever and was missing from the week,
  the Shape Map and the report. It is now finished with the sets she logged.
- A Home Screen app resumed the next morning still showed "Session in progress" and logged her
  new sets into yesterday's workout. On a new day it now reloads (a workout started in the last
  three hours — one running across midnight — is left alone).
- Backups held no photos (a Blob becomes `{}` in JSON), and restoring one broke the Shape screen.
  Photos now travel as data URLs; an old backup's empty photo is skipped.
- If iOS closed the database while the app was in the background, the next set was lost. The
  write is retried once on a fresh connection.
- The backup download link expired before iPhone's "Download?" prompt; a crafted backup could
  run code in five places (now escaped).

**Showing the right thing**
- Today announced a deload week ("about 60% of the load") that nothing applied, and the program
  description promised one every fifth or sixth week. Both claims are gone; deloads are an open
  decision below.
- "Remind me on this phone" saved a setting nothing read, so nothing ever reminded her. Removed;
  the booking card on Today is the reminder.
- After finishing, Today still offered "Start warm-up", and after logging a class it still
  offered "I went" — a second tap made a duplicate.
- "Move it" only moved the session on Week; Today, Train, Shape and the day sheet kept the old
  day. "Dismiss" on a warning did nothing. A class added from a day's sheet could be saved as a
  one-off on a different date.
- The Measurements card went blank after a photo or an InBody scan; waist-to-hip showed one
  decimal (0.758 as "0.8"); inches lost their quarters (40.25 as "40.3″"); Me showed a raw date
  and "Last backed up 0 days ago"; "1 sets · 1 exercises"; "5 × 5–5" and "1 × 10–20".

**Train**
- "+15 s" added 30; the rest setting in Me did nothing (it now defaults to "As planned" and a
  chosen time applies after every set); clearing a field in Edit set saved "—"; reopening after
  the app was closed always went back to the first exercise.
- The warm-up's timer kept painting over whichever tab she moved to, and finishing it from there
  started a session.

**Navigation**
- Every refresh — a readiness tap, a logged set, the theme — jumped the page back to the top.
- Tapping two tabs quickly could leave one tab's content under the other's highlight.
- Closing the share sheet on "Send myself the list" went on to copy the list and open Messages;
  tapping the mic to stop listening said "Could not listen just now".

**Layout on small phones and at large text**
- Onboarding's Continue/Skip footer floated 100–134 px above the bottom and covered the options.
- The expanded set row's weight field showed "1" for 165 lb on an iPhone 14; the pinned row sat
  off-centre with the Swap button showing through, carried empty home-indicator padding, and left
  its links half under the tab bar; the session bar let scrolling text show above it; the Swap
  button hung off the card.
- Labels were cut with "…" ("165 lb × …", button labels, class-picker warnings); they wrap now.
- The rest timer's progress track was 0 px wide, and its button read "Ski…".

## Open — decisions, not bugs

- **Deloads.** The life stages set how often a lighter week should come, but nothing schedules
  one, and a lighter week logged by hand would hold the next week's targets back. Same in Lady_GYM.
- At the largest text sizes the expanded set row is taller than the space above the tab bar and
  needs a short scroll (the compact row, the default, always fits); on a 320pt phone the tab
  labels shorten to "Tod…". Capping the tab-label size, as iOS does, would be a design change.
- Swapping an exercise mid-workout hides its logged sets until Finish (nothing is lost).
- Personal bests, "Moving up" and Strength ignore lifts done above 12 reps (the engine's 1RM
  estimate stops at 12).
- Train still offers today's planned session after it is done, as the way to start another.

## Re-running the checks

Daybreak has no test runner of its own; the Playwright scripts used here lived in the session's
scratch space. Before sending an update to people, open it once on a real iPhone: start a
session, log two sets, put the phone to sleep for a minute, and open it again.
