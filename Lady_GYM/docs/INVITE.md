# Invite text: copy, paste, send

Send this as a **text message** (iMessage/SMS) or **email**. Links in Messages and Mail open
straight in Safari, which can install the app. Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp open links in
their own built-in browser instead, which adds an extra step. The install page handles that
case, but a text message avoids it.

The link only works once the pull request is merged into `main` and GitHub Pages has finished
publishing, usually within a few minutes.

---

## Text message

> Hi! 💪 I'd love for you to try **Lady_GYM**, a free workout app made for women, with a
> picture for every exercise. It plans your lifting around your Bay Club classes.
>
> It takes about 30 seconds to put on your iPhone. No App Store, no sign-up, no password:
>
> 1. Tap this link (it opens in Safari):
>    https://sal-padilla.github.io/daybreak/ladygym
> 2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow ⬆️) at the bottom of the screen.
>    On the newest iPhones, tap **•••** first, then **Share**.
> 3. Scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
> 4. Open the pink **Lady_GYM** icon on your Home Screen and tap **Get Started**.
>
> The page shows a picture of every step too. Everything stays private on your phone.
>
> Try one workout and tell me what you think! 😊

## If they get stuck (a follow-up text)

> - **It opened inside Instagram, Facebook or Gmail?** Tap **•••** at the top right, choose
>   **Open in Safari**, then do steps 2–3.
> - **Can't find "Add to Home Screen"?** It's further down the Share list. Scroll down.
> - **Android phone?** Open the link in Chrome, tap **⋮** at the top right, then
>   **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
> - **Already added it?** Open it from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari. That's where your
>   workouts are saved.
